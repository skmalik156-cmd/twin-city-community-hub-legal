Contact and support

In the app

The quickest way to reach us is the Help form on the sign-in screen of the app. It works even if you cannot sign in. Use it for support questions, problems with your account, and account deletion requests.

Inside the app, the Suggestions section sends your feedback directly to the administrator, and you can report any post or member that breaks the rules.

Public questions and bug reports

You can open an issue on our public page: https://github.com/skmalik156-cmd/twin-city-community-hub-legal/issues. This is public, so never post personal information (e-mail, phone number, passwords) there.
