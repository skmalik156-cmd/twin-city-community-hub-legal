Delete your Twin City Community Hub account

You can delete your account and the data linked to it in two ways.

Option 1 - inside the app (recommended)

  1. Open Twin City Community Hub and sign in.
  2. Open My Profile.
  3. Tap DELETE MY ACCOUNT and confirm with your username.

Your account is frozen straight away and is deleted after a short waiting period, during which you can cancel the request from the app.

Option 2 - if you cannot sign in or no longer have the app

  1. Install the app, or open it, and on the sign-in screen tap Help.
  2. In the form, enter your name, the e-mail address of the account and the message "Please delete my account" together with your username, then send it. This works without signing in.
  3. An administrator will match the e-mail address and username with the account and delete it. For your security, an account is only deleted when the request matches the account; if we cannot match it we will not delete the account.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, posts, comments, likes, saved items, reviews, reports and blocks
  • Your direct messages, blood-donor and professional listings
  • Your uploaded photos and CV files, and your notification tokens
  • Your sign-in account (e-mail address and password hash)

What is kept, and for how long

A minimal record that a deletion was requested and completed (it contains no content) is kept for up to 90 days for security and abuse prevention. If there is an open abuse report or a legal obligation, deletion can be paused and we will tell you. See the Privacy Policy.
