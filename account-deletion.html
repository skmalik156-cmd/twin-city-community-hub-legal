Delete your Twin City Community Hub account

To protect members, an account is only deleted when the request is made from that account itself. We never delete an account because someone supplies an e-mail address or username: both can be known or guessed by other people.

How to delete your account

  1. Open Twin City Community Hub and sign in.
  2. Open My Profile.
  3. Tap DELETE MY ACCOUNT and confirm with your username.

Your account is frozen straight away and is deleted after a short waiting period. You can cancel the request from the app during that period.

Delete from a web browser

You can also request deletion without the app on our separate, secure deletion page: https://twin-city-account-deletion.pages.dev/account-deletion. You sign in there with your own e-mail address and password. It works the same way as in the app: your account is frozen at once and deleted after the waiting period, and you can cancel from the app or the same page during that time. We never ask for your password on this legal website.

If you cannot sign in

Reinstall the app and sign in with your e-mail address and password. If you forgot your password, use Forgot password on the sign-in screen when it is available for your account. If you still cannot sign in, tell us through the Help form on the sign-in screen. We will not delete an account unless we can confirm it belongs to the person asking; a request we cannot confirm is not processed.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, posts, comments, likes, saved items, reviews written by or about you, and your blocks
  • Your direct messages: conversations are removed for you and for the other member
  • Your blood-donor and professional listings, your uploaded photos and CV files, and your notification tokens
  • Your sign-in account (e-mail address and password hash)

What is kept, and for how long

  • Reports you made about other members' posts are kept for moderation, without your identity.
  • Feedback you sent through Suggestions is kept for the administrator without your name or username.
  • Pending notifications to or from you are removed.
  • A minimal record that a deletion was requested and completed (it contains no content) is kept for up to 90 days for security and abuse prevention. If there is a legal obligation, deletion can be paused and we will tell you. See the Privacy Policy.
