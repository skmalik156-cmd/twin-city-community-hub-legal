Child safety standards

Last updated: 24 October 2026. These standards apply to the Android app Twin City Community Hub (package com.skmalik.twincityhub), developed by SK Malik.

Our position

We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). The app is for adults aged 18 and over (see the Terms of Use) and is not intended for children.

What is prohibited

  • Any content that sexualises, exploits or endangers a child, in posts, photos, profiles or messages.
  • Grooming, soliciting, or arranging contact with a child for sexual purposes, and trading or requesting CSAM.
  • Using the app to find, contact or harm children in any other way.

How we prevent and respond

  • Reporting: every post can be reported from inside the app with a written reason; post reports are stored for the developer to review. The app does not yet have a button to report a member or a single private message: for those, write to the contact address below and include the member’s username.
  • Blocking: any member can be blocked from inside the app.
  • Moderation: city moderators can reject posts, and the developer can remove content and suspend or close accounts. Jobs, rentals, property and announcements are reviewed before they appear, and photos on posts that are waiting for approval are visible only to the author and the city moderators.
  • Action: content that breaches these standards is removed and the account is suspended or closed. Apparent child sexual abuse material (CSAM) is removed and the account is closed. Confirmed CSAM is reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (CyberTipline, report.cybertip.org) and, as applicable, to Pakistan’s National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency (NCCIA; helpline 1799). Anyone can also report it directly to the police or those authorities.
  • Limits: private messages can be read only by the sender and the receiver and are not scanned by us. The app is run by an individual developer, so responses may take time; we act on serious reports as quickly as we reasonably can.

How to make a complaint, and what happens next

  1. Tell us. Use Report on the post, or e-mail sk.malik159@gmail.com (put “Twin City child safety” in the subject) with the username, what you saw and, if you can, where (city, category, post title). Do not forward or copy illegal images: describe where they are instead.
  2. We review. Reports about children are looked at before other reports.
  3. We act. Content that breaks these standards is removed and the account is suspended or closed. Apparent CSAM is removed, the account is closed and confirmed CSAM is reported as described below.
  4. Not satisfied? Reply to the same e-mail and ask for the decision to be looked at again. You can always also contact your local police or child-protection authority directly; in an emergency, contact your local emergency services first.

Evidence and reporting to authorities

  • We do not copy, download, forward or screenshot suspected CSAM, because handling it can itself be illegal. We record identifiers only (post, member, city, category and time) and look at the content in the app only as far as needed to confirm it.
  • Removal and reporting: when we confirm CSAM we remove it, close the account, and make a report to the authorities named above with those identifiers.
  • Legal requests: if a competent authority asks us to preserve or provide records about a report, we will respond as the law requires. Account deletion can be paused while such a request is open.
  • Not everything is automatic: the app does not scan photos or messages; we rely on member reports and moderator review.
