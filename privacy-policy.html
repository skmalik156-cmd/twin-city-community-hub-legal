Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026. This policy covers the Android app Twin City Community Hub (package com.skmalik.twincityhub), developed by SK Malik. Questions: see Contact.

1. What the app collects and why

  • Account details: your e-mail address and password. The password is stored only as a salted hash by our authentication provider. We use these to create and secure your account.
  • Profile: your name, a username, your city (Rawalpindi or Islamabad) and an optional profile photo. Other members can see them.
  • Content you choose to share: posts and the photos you attach, comments, likes, saved posts, reviews, reports, blocks and direct messages.
  • Optional services: if you create a blood-donor or blood-request listing we store the name, phone number, blood group and location you enter; if you create a professional listing we store the business details, phone number, work photos and CV file you upload. Phone numbers are shown only on the listing you publish.
  • Support requests: the name, e-mail address and message you send through the in-app Help form.
  • Notifications: if notifications are enabled and you allow them, a push-notification token for your device.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera or microphone, shows no advertising, contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not sell personal data.

2. Who can see your information

  • Your name, username, city, profile photo and approved posts are visible to other members of the app.
  • Photos attached to a post that is still waiting for approval, or was rejected, can be viewed only by you and by the moderators of that city.
  • Direct messages can be read only by the sender and the receiver inside the app. Reports can be read by the person who made the report and by the moderators of that city.
  • Administrators and moderators can review and remove content that is reported or that breaks the community rules.
  • The developer has technical access to the database for maintenance and security, subject to the protections described below.

3. Service providers

We use Supabase to provide the database, sign-in and file storage (the project is hosted in the Mumbai, India region). When push notifications are switched on, notification delivery uses Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. These providers process data on our behalf to run the service.

4. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time inside the app (My Profile → Delete my account), or follow the steps on the account deletion page. Deletion removes your profile, posts, comments, messages, listings, uploaded files, notification tokens and your sign-in account. A minimal record that a deletion was requested and completed (it contains no content) is kept for up to 90 days for security and abuse prevention. A deletion can be paused if there is an open abuse report or a legal obligation; we will tell you if that happens.

5. Security

Data travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Every database table is protected by row-level security rules so members can only reach data they are allowed to see. Passwords are hashed. No online service is perfectly secure, so please choose a strong, unique password (at least 8 characters).

6. Your choices

  • You can view and edit your profile and delete your own posts inside the app.
  • You can turn off notifications in your phone settings.
  • You can ask questions about your data through the contact options.

7. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us and we will remove it.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page and, for important changes, tell members in the app.
